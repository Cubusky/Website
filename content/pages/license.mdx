## Cubusky Code

All open source projects are publicly available on the [Cubusky GitHub][github] and licensed under the [MIT License][mit], which means that it's free to use in all commercial- or non-commercial projects with no attribution required (though a mention is always appreciated).

Our closed source projects are visible only to our members and licensed under our own [Cubusky License](), which states that any package you purchase receives updates for a year, after which you own that version and any patches it receives.

> - Package version 1.0.0 purchased on 1-1-2000 
>   - You own version 1.0.0
> - Version 1.1.0 released on 2-2-2000 (within a year)
>   - You own version 1.1.0
> - Version 1.2.0 released on 1-1-2001 (after a year)
>   - You do NOT own version 1.2.0
> - Version 1.1.1 released on 10-10-2010 (security patch)
