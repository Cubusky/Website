**Cubusky** is pronounced **"Cube" "Husky"** without the h. **Cubusky!**

Other than it's pronunciation, Cubusky is an organization aimed at developing tools for game developers in the [C# programming language][c#]. Our products are available for every C# application, and our support stretches to every Game Engine running C# (even custom-build engines!) At the core of our philosophy is the separation of a games visual elements with its logic and functionality, and that separation of concerns allows us the insight to build highly generic systems.

With strong roots in open source communities, we try to provide a healthy balance between open source tools that lay the structural foundation of our packages, and more high-level products aimed at empowering the needs of professional studios and creatives. We also offer support for both our commercial and open source tools, and can be hired as [Chickensoft] consultants (more on that later).

We believe open source is essential to fostering a healthy game development environment where everybody gets to be on equal footing. Games are a huge form of self expression, and where we are able we want to empower creatives, students, teachers and anyone unable to afford or invest in game development tools to be able to build good games.

# Chickensoft

Chickensoft is a volunteer organization dedicated to empowering developers who use the open source [Godot game engine][Godot] with the [C# programming language][c#], and they have tremendously inspired our philosophy about game architecture. We've adopted their [Philosophy on Game Code][Philosophy] wholeheartedly and recommend you read through it if you'd like to learn more.
